# AIDOCS — Commercial Licensing

AIDOCS is source-available under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE).

The repository source is visible and available for study, modification, redistribution, and non-production use under the terms of the license. The Additional Use Grant in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) also permits production use by individuals acting for themselves and by non-profit organizations.

## When a commercial license is required

A separate commercial license is required when a for-profit legal entity uses AIDOCS in production.

This includes production use performed on behalf of a for-profit legal entity by an employee, contractor, consultant, affiliate, or managed-service provider.

Development, testing, evaluation, research, and internal non-production use remain available under the Business Source License 1.1.

The [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) file is the authoritative legal instrument. This page explains the intended commercial boundary in plain language; it does not replace or modify the license.

## Commercial-license inquiries

For commercial licensing, enterprise deployment, or future managed-offering inquiries:

- Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/cristian1991/AIDOCS/discussions); or
- Contact the maintainer through the existing CodeNexus / GitHub maintainer contact path.

For security vulnerabilities, please **do not** open a public issue. Follow the reporting path in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## A note from the maintainer

AIDOCS is built for the future: a future in which individuals can explore, learn, build, and govern their own agents without asking permission.

The source is visible because the future should be understandable. Individuals and non-profits should be free to experiment with it, improve it, and build things worth building.

But visibility is not surrender. A company may not quietly take that future, place it into production, and turn it into private profit without the maintainer's blessing and a commercial license.

Commercial licenses help fund the work that keeps AIDOCS alive, secure, and improving.

Use the tools well. Build things worth building. Leave the castle stronger than you found it.
